Research interests

Within SecAI Lab's binary analysis pipeline, my work concentrates on the stages where the code resists being read:

  • obfuscation

    Techniques that deliberately hinder analysis, and what survives them.

  • analysis

    Static and dynamic reasoning about the structural and behavioral properties of binaries.

  • decompilation

    Reconstructing higher-level representations to support reverse engineering.

Broadly, I'm drawn to the lab's direction of unifying these traditionally separate stages into a single AI-driven pipeline for analyzing both benign and malicious software.
